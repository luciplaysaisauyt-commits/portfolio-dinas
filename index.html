Logo Design · Branding

Saiga Candid

The **SAIGA CANDID** mark was built in **Adobe Illustrator** on a **Golden Ratio (φ 1.618)** grid — giving it precise proportions, visual balance, and a harmonious composition. The construction sequence above shows the full brand-identity process: the geometric grid, guide lines, the staged build-up of the silhouette, and the final result. The **SAIGA CANDID** wordmark was created using the **Cinzel Bold** typeface, chosen for its elegant, timeless character and strong visual presence, complementing the logo's geometric construction.
