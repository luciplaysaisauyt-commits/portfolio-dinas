Project Overview

“Set it and forget it” dairy delivery

DairyDrop is a subscription-based dairy and non-dairy delivery service that gives customers the convenience of a grocery run — without the trip. The goal was a validated, low-friction flow built around three things: search, transparency, and effortless subscription control.

The approach combined competitive research, primary user interviews, and quantitative market data to move from assumptions about the target user to a design grounded in real behaviour.

The Challenge

How might we make dairy delivery reliable and flexible?

How might we

Help customers achieve reliable, flexible dairy delivery without dealing with unpredictable delivery windows and confusing subscription management?

The Challenges
01
Unpredictable delivery windows
Customers skip entire weeks when they can’t plan around uncertain arrival times.
02
Confusing subscription management
No clear path to pause, skip, or modify a recurring order — leading to cancellations instead of adjustments.
03
Lack of origin transparency
Customers wanted to know which farm their milk came from — the existing UI gave no traceability.
04
High new-user friction
Account creation was required before product browsing — a well-known driver of top-of-funnel abandonment.
My Role & Vision

Sole designer, end-to-end

End-to-end ownership of research, strategy, interaction design and visual design. The design goal was invisibility — every decision measured against one question: does this remove a step, or add one?

My responsibilities

  • Competitive analysis of four direct competitors across ten feature dimensions
  • Primary qualitative research: walk-and-talk interview with the target user
  • Secondary quantitative research: market sizing, delivery-abandonment data, food price indices
  • Assumptive and validated personas, empathy maps and user flows
  • Information architecture and sitemap
  • Speed sketching, A/B testing, low-, mid- and high-fidelity wireframes
  • Customer journey map
  • Interactive prototype and brand-aligned UI system

Project vision

To build a dairy delivery app that feels as considered as the product it delivers — where a customer can find what they need in one search, understand exactly where their milk came from, know precisely when it will arrive, and skip a week in a single tap.

Success criteria

  1. A first-time user reaches a product page without creating an account
  2. Any product is findable in one search
  3. Subscription pause or skip in a single tap
  4. Delivery ETA visible before checkout

Reflection

What the research changed

01
Being proven wrong
The assumptive persona expected a sustainability-driven user. Observation showed the opposite — she skipped the ethical story and looked for a farm name and best-before date. Proof over narrative.
02
Sign-in placement matters
A sign-in wall right after the splash made me abandon a competitor app almost immediately. Guest browsing became non-negotiable.
03
Cancel-ability = trust
Subscription, assumed to be a simple benefit, triggered the strongest emotion: fear of not being able to cancel. Easy pause/skip earns the subscription.
04
Watching beats asking
Users described themselves as browsers — then went straight to the search bar every time. Design for what people do, not what they say.
