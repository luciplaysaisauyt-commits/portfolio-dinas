PROJECT OVERVIEW

Banking, redesigned for the mobile generation

Nova Bank is a concept fintech mobile banking app designed around clarity, speed, and trust. The goal was to simplify the everyday banking experience by making balances, transfers, cards, and transaction history easier to understand at a glance.

Since this is a concept project without access to real Nova Bank users, I built the primary persona from patterns observed across competitor reviews, app store feedback, and common friction points reported by neobank users — then mapped her thoughts, feelings, and emotional curve through the send-money flow so every design decision traces back to a realistic need rather than a guess.

USER RESEARCH

What users actually hate about banking apps

Before designing a single screen, I analyzed competitor apps and identified the friction points that make users frustrated with traditional banking UX.

Navigation overload

Too many tabs, buried features, inconsistent IA. Users can't find what they need fast.

Trust & clarity gap

Hidden fees, confusing transaction labels, and unclear balances erode user confidence.

Slow critical actions

Sending money or checking a statement takes too many steps. Users expect instant feedback.

Outdated visual design

Many banking apps feel visually outdated compared to modern consumer products. Users expect a cleaner, more premium digital experience.

TARGET AUDIENCE

Who Nova Bank is designed for

Nova Bank targets mobile-first users who already bank with — or have switched away from — neobanks like Revolut, Monzo, or Chime. They expect the speed and clarity of those apps, but want a visual identity that feels more premium than the typical neobank palette.

Young professionals (22–35)

Salary, daily spending, and savings managed almost entirely from a phone — minimal patience for friction.

Neobank-fluent users

Already use Revolut, Monzo, or Chime daily; benchmark every new fintech app against that bar.

Frequent transactors

Send money, split bills, and check balances multiple times a day — every extra tap is a cost.

Design-conscious users

Want their banking app to feel as considered as the rest of the apps on their phone — not generic fintech green/blue.

COMPETITOR RESEARCH

How Nova Bank compares

I benchmarked Nova Bank against three neobanks widely recognized for strong UX — Revolut, Monzo, and Chime — to find the gap Nova Bank could realistically own.

Feature Revolut Monzo Chime Nova Bank
Instant transaction notifications
Budgeting / savings pots
Card freeze / unfreeze in 1 tap
Premium / non-generic visual identity
Designed error & failure states
Transfer flow in ≤3 steps
Multi-currency / travel features
Revolut, Monzo, and Chime have already solved speed and core functionality — that bar is table stakes, not a differentiator. Where they consistently fall short is visual identity (most lean on generic fintech color systems) and designed edge cases: failed transfers, security alerts, and incorrect-PIN states are often an afterthought. Nova Bank's opportunity is a premium dark UI paired with fully designed system states, not a wider feature set.

Problem and goals

Problem

Traditional banking apps prioritize compliance and feature completeness over user experience. The result is cluttered interfaces with poor hierarchy, confusing navigation, and visual design that creates anxiety rather than confidence. Users distrust what they can't read clearly.

Goals

Design a mobile banking app that makes balance, transactions, and transfers immediately clear. Create a dark premium UI system that communicates trust and modernity. Reduce cognitive load so every action takes fewer taps and zero confusion.

How Might We

"How might we design a banking app that feels as intuitive and premium as the best consumer apps, while maintaining the clarity and trust that financial products demand?"

KEY LEARNINGS

What I learned building Nova Bank

01
Trust is a design decision
Every color, spacing choice, and label in a financial product either builds or erodes user confidence. Premium dark UI with clear hierarchy communicates reliability.
02
Design systems compound
Investing in a full token-based system upfront paid off massively. Every new screen was 3× faster to build because components were already defined.
03
Mobile constraints drive creativity
The limited viewport forces clear priority decisions. What stays on screen, what goes behind a tap — every choice reveals what actually matters to the user.
04
Empty states are features
Designing every loading, error, and empty state turns edge cases into moments of delight — or at minimum, moments of clarity rather than confusion.
05
A/B testing beats assumptions
Testing the card layout against the list layout, and the single-step confirm against the two-step review, showed that real user behavior didn't always match my initial instinct — the data settled the debate.
